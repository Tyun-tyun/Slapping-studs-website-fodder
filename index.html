Chapter 01 · 1982

Some students,
looking for action

The Slapping Studs were not founded as a project or a plan. A group of students at the University of Twente wanted something faster than what the campus offered, found the ice rink, and started showing up. Four decades later that is still roughly how new members arrive.

"We're an association first and a team second. The competition results are what happens along the way."

Chapter 04 · 2026

The Striking Stallions went into the third period of the decisive game a goal short and finished it 4–3, in front of supporters who had travelled with the team. The first divisional title in the club's history.

Team / About us

Competitive,
not serious

SIJHV The Slapping Studs is a student ice hockey association affiliated with the University of Twente since 1982. We've done well in competition, but the results don't come before the association — hanging out and having fun is an important part of every Dutch student team, and it's an important part of this one.

Members

One coach and around 50 players, from complete beginners to advanced. More than a quarter are international students, which is why English is the working language on the ice and on this site. Membership requires a University of Twente Campus Card.

The board

Four board positions — chairman, secretary, treasurer and game coordinator — elected each year, plus an Off-Ice Committee that runs the events calendar and a webmaster who keeps this site standing.

Team / Training

Twice a week,
October to March

Practice covers skating, stick handling, tactics and rules. The ice is split into groups by level, so a first-timer and a player of ten years get a session that fits. All training is at IJsbaan Twente in Enschede.

Hockey / For people who've never played

The fastest
team sport there is

Six players a side — three forwards, two defenders, a goaltender — on a 60 × 30 m sheet of ice, three periods of 20 minutes, shifts of 45 seconds because nobody can skate flat out for longer than that. The puck is 170 g of vulcanised rubber and moves faster than anything else in team sport.
