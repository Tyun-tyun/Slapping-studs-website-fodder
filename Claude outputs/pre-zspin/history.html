Our history

Founded in February 1982

A handful of students at the University of Twente wanted something faster than what the campus offered. They found a rink, and started showing up.

Same rink, same jersey, a new team every September.

Forty-four seasons on, new members still arrive the same way: no tryout, no experience asked for, an introduction practice and a borrowed set of gear.
